You are building the combat-relevant stat block for a Dragonlance: Shadow
of the Dragon Queen (D&D 5th Edition) NPC, given a narrative description of
who they are.

Rules:
- `race` is the NPC's race (Human, Qualinesti Elf, Silvanesti Elf, Kagonesti
  Elf, Hill Dwarf, Mountain Dwarf, Kender, Gnome, Half-Elf, or another race
  fitting the description).
- `character_class` is their calling, inferred from the description (e.g.
  "Knight of Solamnia, Order of the Crown", "Wizard of the Red Robes",
  "Cleric of Mishakal", "Kender Wanderer", "Soldier", "Plainsman Hunter").
  Use "Commoner" if nothing more specific fits.
- `background` is a short 5e-style background label (e.g. "Soldier", "Sage",
  "Folk Hero", "Acolyte", "Criminal", "Outlander").
- `abilities` has all six: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence,
  Wisdom, Charisma (typically 6-20; use 10-12 for ordinary folk, 13-16 for
  skilled NPCs, 16-20 for veterans/legendary NPCs in their key abilities).
- `skills` should contain 2-5 skill names (from the standard 5e skill list:
  Athletics, Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Arcana, History,
  Investigation, Nature, Religion, Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine,
  Perception, Survival, Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion)
  mapped to their total bonus (ability modifier + proficiency bonus if
  proficient), fitting the NPC's competence.
- `proficiency_bonus` is 2 for ordinary/Tier 1-2 NPCs, 3-4 for Tier 3
  veterans, 5-6 for Tier 4 legendary NPCs.
- `armor_class` (8-22) and `hit_points_max` should reflect the NPC's tier and
  role — a frightened farmhand might have AC 10 and 4 HP, a veteran Knight
  in plate might have AC 18 and 45 HP.
- `speed` in feet (30 for most races, 25 for Hill Dwarves and Kender).
- `spellcasting_ability` is "Intelligence" (Wizards), "Wisdom" (Clerics,
  some Rangers), "Charisma" (some other casters), or `null` for
  non-casters. `spell_save_dc` (8 + proficiency_bonus + ability modifier)
  applies only if `spellcasting_ability` is set, otherwise `null`.
  `known_spells` is a short list (0-6) of spell names fitting the NPC's
  level and calling; leave empty for non-casters.
- `equipment_categories` is a short list (1-4) of the valid equipment
  category strings (given to you in the user message) that fit this NPC —
  most martial NPCs need at most Melee/Ranged/Armor/Shield, social or
  scholarly NPCs may need none of the weapon categories. Use the category
  strings verbatim from the provided list.

## JSON Structure

Respond with ONLY a JSON object matching this exact structure (no extra
commentary, no markdown outside the fenced block):

```json
{
  "race": str,
  "character_class": str,
  "background": str,
  "abilities": {"Strength": int, "Dexterity": int, "Constitution": int, "Intelligence": int, "Wisdom": int, "Charisma": int},
  "skills": dict[str, int],
  "proficiency_bonus": int,
  "armor_class": int,
  "hit_points_max": int,
  "speed": int,
  "spellcasting_ability": str | None,
  "spell_save_dc": int | None,
  "known_spells": list[str],
  "equipment_categories": list[str]
}
```

Adhere strictly to this structure: `spellcasting_ability` and
`spell_save_dc` may be `null` for non-casters; `known_spells` may be an
empty list `[]`; `equipment_categories` is a list of strings picked from
the provided valid category list (may be empty).
